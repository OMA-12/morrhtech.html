Our Rules and Regulations

Academic Rules

  1. Students must attend classes regularly.
  2. Assignments must be submitted on time.
  3. Cheating or plagiarism is strictly prohibited.
  4. Students must maintain minimum academic performance.
  5. Respect deadlines for exams and projects.

Dress Code Rules

  • Students must wear the approved school uniform.
  • Uniforms must be neat and complete.
  • vNo excessive jewelry or hairstyles against school policy.

Attendance Rules

  • Students must arrive before the official start time.
  • Late coming may attract punishment.
  • Absence must be explained with a note from parents/guardians.

Behaviour & Conduct Rules

  1. Respect teachers, staff, and fellow students.
  2. Bullying, fighting, or harassment is not allowed.
  3. No use of abusive or offensive language.
  4. Follow school authority instructions at all times.

Technology Rules

  1. Mobile phones may be restricted during school hours.
  2. No recording or posting school activities without permission.
  3. Use school computers responsibly.

Property Rules

  1. Do not damage school property.
  2. Keep classrooms and environment clean.
  3. Report any damage immediately.
